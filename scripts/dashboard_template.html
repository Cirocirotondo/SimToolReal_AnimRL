What the numbers mean

Mean step reward
Sum of the imitation and object terms, per control step. The ceiling with the current weights is about 2.4.
Evaluation score
Deterministic replay from fixed and sampled RSI frames, always unassisted. This is what picks best_model.pt. Do not compare it between runs. It is computed on the fixed cohort and subtracts that cohort's early-termination fraction outright, and it is built from Gaussian reward terms — so changing a reward sigma moves the score of an unchanged policy. Use the two cohorts and the scale-free numbers below instead.
Trained starts vs fixed starts
Every evaluation runs two cohorts. Trained starts follow the RSI distribution the run actually trains on, and are the honest measure of the policy. Fixed starts replay four phases of the demonstration — frames 0, 276, 553 and 830 — and pregrasp_mixture concentrates 80% of its training in [740, 830], so three of the four begin where the policy is barely trained. A whole cohort failing is exactly 16 of 64 episodes, which is why that number sticks at 25%.
Vibration (rms action rate)
Root-mean-square step-to-step change in the commanded action. Independent of every reward sigma, so it is the one smoothness number comparable across all runs. The base blind run sits at 2.53; sharpen_sigma, the smoothest policy on the box, reaches 0.008. Under about 0.1 is where a policy stops shaking hard enough to worry a real joint.
Cube lift
Peak height of the cube's centre of mass above where that episode reset it. Under 20 mm means it never really left the table.
Early termination
Fraction of episodes killed by the arm/hand tracking thresholds instead of reaching the horizon.
Action std
Policy exploration width. The divergence guard aborts the run at 15.
Two kinds of clip
Training clips are 10 s of one environment mid-rollout, every 500 updates. The evaluation replay is the whole demonstration played deterministically at the end of the run, with the green reference robot beside the policy robot.
Object-assist scale
Strength of the external PD wrench helping the cube along the demonstration. It decays to zero, so the policy has to take over.
